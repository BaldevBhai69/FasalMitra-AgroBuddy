OUR MISSION

FasalMitra is dedicated to revolutionizing Indian agriculture by placing intelligent, hyper-localized agronomy directly into the hands of every farmer. By synthesizing real-time IoT soil intelligence, satellite telemetry, and predictive weather forecasting with FAO-56 irrigation protocols and automated disease diagnosis, we eliminate guesswork from cultivation. Our mission is to safeguard vital groundwater reserves, drastically reduce input overheads, and deliver equitable, transparent APMC mandi price discovery—transforming every acre into a resilient, high-yielding, and financially sovereign harvest for generations to come.

OUR TECH

IoT Soil Telemetry

Real-time NPK, soil moisture, and pH telemetry streamed continuously from low-power sub-GHz field probes.

FAO-56 Irrigation Engine

Automated Penman-Monteith evapotranspiration algorithms calculating precision water requirements per crop stage.

AI Vision Pathology

Edge-computed Computer Vision diagnosing foliar diseases, blights, and pest infestations in milliseconds.

Satellite NDVI Telemetry

Multispectral Sentinel-2 satellite telemetry tracking chlorophyll density, canopy moisture, and biomass health.

APMC Mandi Intelligence

Predictive neural networks forecasting commodity spot prices, arrival volumes, and peak selling windows across India.

Hyper-Local Microclimate

Sub-kilometer numerical weather prediction synthesizing global ECMWF forecasts with live on-farm micro-sensors.
